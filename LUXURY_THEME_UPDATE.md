## 🎨 Customization Options

Want to adjust the luxury theme? Here's what you can easily change:

### Gold Shade:
Change `#FFD700` to your preferred gold tone in `styles.css`

### Background:
Adjust the black shades in the `body` background

### Glow Intensity:
Modify the `rgba(255, 215, 0, X)` values for stronger/softer glows

### Animation Speed:
Adjust animation durations in CSS keyframes
