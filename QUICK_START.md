# 🚀 Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 Want to use it RIGHT NOW? (No deployment needed)

1. **Double-click `index.html`** - Opens in your browser
2. **Fill in the form** with birthday details
3. **Click "Generate Magic Link"**
4. **Click "Preview Magic"** to test it locally

⚠️ **Note**: Links will only work on YOUR computer until you deploy online.

---

## 🌍 Want links to work WORLDWIDE? (2 minutes!)

### Easiest Method - Netlify:

1. Go to **https://www.netlify.com**
2. Sign up (free)
3. **Drag and drop** your BIRTHDAY folder
4. Get your link: `https://yoursite.netlify.app`
5. Done! 🎉

Now generate links and they'll work anywhere in the world!

---

## 📱 How to Create a Birthday Link

1. **Open your website** (locally or deployed)
2. **Choose a preset** (Dad, Mom, etc.) or enter custom name
3. **Add a personal message** (optional)
4. **Add your name** in "From" field
5. **Click "Generate Magic Link"**
6. **Share via**:
   - WhatsApp 📱
   - Messenger 💬
   - Email 📧
   - Copy & paste anywhere 📋
